Smart Adds & Pre-Trip Checks

Humboldt / Redwood Add-Ons

  • Fern Canyon + Gold Bluffs Beach — a bigger Day 2 adventure if everyone wants redwoods plus coast. Expect gravel road, stream crossings, and wet feet. Day-use reservations usually apply May 15–Sept 15, so your May 8 date is before that window, but still check conditions.
  • Newton B. Drury Scenic Parkway — easier redwood wow than a long hike, and pairs well with Prairie Creek if you want a classic old-growth drive.
  • Arcata Marsh — low-effort, close to Bayside/Arcata, good for stretching legs, birds, and sunset if the coast is foggy.

Yosemite Reality Check

  • No 2026 entrance reservation is required, but Yosemite still recommends arriving before 9 AM or after 5 PM to avoid traffic and parking pain.
  • Glacier Point Road and Tioga Road are not guaranteed in early May. Keep Glacier Point as a bonus; if closed, use Valley View, Cook's Meadow, Lower Yosemite Fall, or Sentinel Bridge instead.
  • Mariposa Grove logistics: most visitors use the free shuttle from the Welcome Plaza when the road is open. Budget extra time if you try to add it after Yosemite Valley.
  • Water safety matters in May. Waterfalls are strong, rocks are slick, and rivers run cold and fast.

Disney / Anaheim Tweaks

  • Confirm park reservations and showtimes in the Disneyland app the night before each park visit. Lightning Lane, entertainment, and dining availability can shift.
  • For World of Color, check the app early for viewing options and plan to be near Paradise Bay well before showtime.
  • Free day fallback: Downtown Disney for low-friction food/shopping; Anaheim Packing House if you want a non-Disney meal hall; pool/rest if the park days are running hot.

Oakhurst / Bass Lake Host Adds

  • Miller’s Landing / Bass Lake — host-recommended lake resort with boat rentals, kayaks, paddle boards, jet skis, fishing boats, pontoons, wake boats, burgers and milkshakes.
  • Yosemite Axe Throwing — Oakhurst activity owned by a local world champion timbersports athlete; family-friendly instruction.
  • Yosemite Mountain Sugar Pine Railroad — steam-powered train through Sierra National Forest logging history.
  • Wild Fig, South Gate Brewery, Plazuelas, Bee’s Bakery, and Clash Cafe — host-recommended food and coffee options around Oakhurst/Coarsegold.

Drive-Day Ideas

  • Bayside to Oakhurst: make the first scenic stop early, then protect the afternoon drive. This is the day most likely to feel longer than it looks.
  • Oakhurst to Anaheim: keep lunch flexible around Fresno or Kettleman City, then push through before Los Angeles traffic thickens.
