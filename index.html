Smart Adds & Pre-Trip Checks

Humboldt / Redwood Add-Ons

  • Easy day: Arcata Plaza, Arcata Marsh, Sequoia Park Zoo / Redwood Sky Walk, and Moonstone Beach or Trinidad Head.
  • Bigger redwoods + coast: Prairie Creek Visitor Center, Newton B. Drury Scenic Parkway, Elk Prairie, Gold Bluffs Beach, and Fern Canyon if the road looks reasonable.
  • Avenue of the Giants: iconic, but save it for a deliberate long-drive choice; it pulls you south before another long southbound travel day.

Yosemite Reality Check

  • Use a Plan A / Plan B mindset. Plan A is Valley + Mariposa Grove. Plan B adds Glacier Point only if the road is open.
  • Glacier Point Road and Tioga Road are not guaranteed in early May. Keep Glacier Point as a bonus; if closed, use Valley View, Cook's Meadow, Lower Yosemite Fall, Sentinel Bridge, and Mirror Lake instead.
  • Mariposa Grove logistics: most visitors use the free shuttle from the Welcome Plaza when the road is open. Budget extra time if you try to add it after Yosemite Valley.
  • Water safety matters in May. Waterfalls are strong, rocks are slick, and rivers run cold and fast.

Disney / Anaheim Tweaks

  • Start standby-first. Do not buy Lightning Lane until after your first park day tells you whether the family needs it.
  • Best Lightning Lane candidate: DCA evening, because the window is short and the must-do rides are concentrated.
  • For World of Color, check the app early for viewing options and plan to be near Paradise Bay well before showtime if it matters to you.
  • Free day modes: Recovery Mode, Local Food Mode, or Mini-Adventure Mode. Pick based on how the family feels that morning.

Drive-Day Ideas

  • Bayside to Oakhurst: make the first scenic stop early, keep lunch flexible, and protect the afternoon drive. This is the day most likely to feel longer than it looks.
  • Oakhurst to Anaheim: keep lunch flexible around Fresno or Kettleman City, then push through before Los Angeles traffic thickens.
